## 🚀 **How to Use (Simple Steps)**

### **Step 1: Select a Team**
1. Look at the left sidebar
2. Click on any team card
3. Selected team gets pink border

### **Step 2: Start Chatting**
1. Type in the message box at bottom
2. Click send button (arrow icon)
3. See messages appear in center

### **Step 3: Chat with Individual Members**
1. Look at right sidebar "Team Members" section
2. Click the chat icon next to any member's name
3. Start private conversation

### **Step 4: Schedule Meetings**
1. Look at "Meetings" section on right
2. Click "Schedule" button
3. Fill in meeting details

### **Step 5: Review or Close**
1. Scroll to bottom of right sidebar
2. Click "Review Team" to rate them
3. Click "Close Match" to end conversation

## 🎨 **Design Principles Applied**

### **1. Progressive Disclosure**
- Show only what users need
- Hide complex options behind simple buttons
- Reveal details when needed

### **2. Visual Hierarchy**
- Most important = largest/most colorful
- Secondary actions = smaller/lighter
- Consistent spacing and sizing

### **3. Familiar Patterns**
- Left sidebar for navigation (like Discord, Slack)
- Center for main content (like WhatsApp)
- Right sidebar for details (like Teams)

### **4. Immediate Feedback**
- Hover effects on buttons
- Clear selection states
- Loading indicators
- Status messages
